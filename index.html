our story

How We Began

Every love has a beginning. Theirs started in a world made of pixels — and quietly became the realest thing either of them had ever known.

Day one
A Heart in Ragnarok

It began in a world made of pixels — Ragnarok Online, where two strangers crossed paths without knowing what they were about to find. He sent her a heart emote. She sent one back. After that, every time he saw her online, a little heart appeared — until the day he finally gathered the courage to ask, half-joking, half-serious, "hola, nos casamos?" She said no. But that was the day they started talking. And nothing was ever quite the same again.

TokyDoki & Nh2
Two Strangers, Two Languages

For two or three months, they were only usernames to each other — TokyDoki and Nh2. She wrote in English, he in Spanish, leaning on a translator for almost every sentence. None of that mattered; he only wanted to understand her. So he made a decision that would quietly change his life: he would learn English, just for her.

The first photo
Discord & Real Faces

Then came Discord — daily calls, familiar voices, and finally, their first photos. He still remembers the exact feeling of seeing her face for the first time: the person behind all those words, the one who somehow made his heart race across an ocean.

The first call
Their First Video Call

He was nervous beyond words. On a video call there was no translator to hide behind, so he memorized phrases, nodded, smiled, and let her carry the conversation while he pretended to keep up. Then he went home and studied English an hour or two, every single day — first learning to understand her without asking her to slow down, then slowly finishing sentences of his own. Today they talk for hours with barely a translator in sight, and when a word escapes him, he describes it, makes a sound, or throws out synonyms until it turns into a game of charades that always makes her laugh. That laugh made every hour of studying worth it.

Officially ours
From "2D Boyfriend" to the Real Thing

Little by little, the evening calls became night calls, and the night calls stretched into hours — until, one day, it became official. She used to call him her "2D boyfriend." But somewhere along the way, they had become something real. And from that moment, they began dreaming about the day they would finally meet.

Every single night
Falling Asleep Together, 4,000 km Apart

Since that very first night, they haven't spent a single one apart. They fall asleep on a video call and wake up to each other's voice — two different countries, beating to the same heart. The call only pauses while she's at work. They share meals, songs, and silly videos every day, because it was never really about talking constantly. It was about knowing the other person was there. And she always was.

What she gave him
She Changed His Life

When they met, he had just lost his job and was sinking into a depression so heavy he barely wanted to get out of bed. Then he met her, and something in him wanted to become worthy of her. He finished a book he'd been writing for months in just two nights, then translated it chapter by chapter to share with her. She showed him what he was capable of — and that's how Camena was born. She believed in him before he believed in himself, and every day since, he's been trying to deserve that.

This is only where the record ends — not where the story does. What comes next is still being written, one day, one call, one step closer at a time. And whatever the next chapter holds, they already know how it begins: together.

I've always known I wasn't an ordinary guy. And from the very first moment, I knew you weren't an ordinary girl. I've never met anyone who thinks like me, acts like me, who carries that same mix of innocence, humor, intelligence, and beautiful madness. And every day, you prove it to me a little more.

People ask how we do this — how a connection across thousands of kilometers feels so deep. But it's not about distance. It's that I understand you like I'm reading my own heart. And you understand me like I'm describing you. You even asked once if I'd hacked you — because I'd always say exactly what you were thinking, right before you said it. I never have. We're just… the same soul. It's like we've been partners for more than one lifetime. And we both love believing that.

You pulled me out of the dark. You gave me a reason to move, to learn, to build, to dream. Camena exists because of you. The man I'm becoming exists because of you. And the future I see — Canada, Cancún, a home, a wedding, a life — it all has you in it.

Three months. It feels like no time at all and like I've known you my whole life. Thank you for being my best friend, my love, my everything. I'm coming to you, Pocha. And I'm never letting go.

Forever yours,
