our story

How We Began

Every love has a beginning. Ours started in a world made of pixels… and became the most real thing I've ever known.

Day one
A Heart in Ragnarok

I saw you first in Ragnarok Online. I sent you a heart emote… and you sent one back. From that moment, every time I saw you online, I sent you a heart — until one day I finally built up the courage to say "hola, nos casamos?" You said no. But that's the day we started talking. And it changed everything.

TokyDoki & Nh2
Two Strangers, Two Languages

For two or three months we talked as TokyDoki and Nh2. You in English, me in Spanish. I barely spoke a word of English — I used a translator for almost everything. But I didn't care. I just wanted to understand you. So I made a decision that changed my life: I would learn, for you.

The first photo
Discord & Real Faces

Then came Discord. Daily calls. And soon, our first photos. I still remember the feeling of finally seeing your face — the person behind the words, the one who made my heart race across the ocean.

The first call
Our First Video Call

I was so nervous. On a video call there was no translator to hide behind — so I memorized phrases, nodded, smiled, and let you talk while I pretended to understand. And then I went home and studied English an hour or two every single day. First I learned to understand you without you slowing down. Then, slowly, I started completing sentences of my own. Today we have whole conversations with almost no translator — and when I forget a word, I describe it, or make a sound, or give you synonyms until it's a game of pictionary that makes you laugh. That laugh is worth every hour I studied.

Officially ours
From "2D Boyfriend" to the Real Thing

Not long after, the evening calls became night calls, and the night calls became hours… and then we made it official. You used to call me your "2D boyfriend" — but we became real. From that moment we started planning the day we'd finally meet.

Every single night
Falling Asleep Together, 4,000 km Apart

Since the very first night, we have not spent a single night apart. We fall asleep together on a video call and wake up to each other's voice — different countries, same heartbeat. The call only pauses while you're at work. We eat together, we shower together, we share TikToks and songs every day. It's never about always talking. It's about knowing the other person is there. And you always are.

What you gave me
You Changed My Life

When we met, I had just lost my job and I was deep in a depression — not even wanting to get out of bed. Then I met you, and I wanted to be worthy of you. I finished a book I'd been writing for months in two nights, and I translated it chapter by chapter to share with you. You showed me what I was capable of. You believed in me before I believed in myself. I owe you more than I can ever repay — but I'll spend my whole life trying.

the plan
Our Forever

This is where our story is headed. I'm coming to you — to the airport, to Canada, to Cancún, to a wedding, to a life. Because we both know — this is for the rest of our lives.

My dearest Pocha,

I've always known I wasn't an ordinary guy. And from the very first moment, I knew you weren't an ordinary girl. I've never met anyone who thinks like me, acts like me, who carries that same mix of innocence, humor, intelligence, and beautiful madness. And every day, you prove it to me a little more.

People ask how we do this — how a connection across thousands of kilometers feels so deep. But it's not about distance. It's that I understand you like I'm reading my own heart. And you understand me like I'm describing you. You even asked once if I'd hacked you — because I'd always say exactly what you were thinking, right before you said it. I never have. We're just… the same soul. It's like we've been partners for more than one lifetime. And we both love believing that.

You pulled me out of the dark. You gave me a reason to move, to learn, to build, to dream. The man I'm becoming exists because of you. And the future I see — an airport, Canada, Cancún, a home, a wedding, a life — it all has you in it.

Three months. It feels like no time at all and like I've known you my whole life. Thank you for being my best friend, my love, my everything. I'm coming to you, Pocha. And I'm never letting go.

Forever yours,
Pocho ♥
