First contact

A heart, because I had nothing better

We met in Ragnarok Online. The first thing I ever sent you was a heart, because I had nothing better to offer than that. You sent one back. For weeks, that was the entire conversation.

Then one day I asked, half as a joke, if you'd marry me. You said no. We have not stopped talking since.

Two languages

Behind a translator

You wrote in English. I wrote in Spanish, badly, with a translator sitting between us like a chaperone. So I learned — not romantically, out of necessity. Every night, a little more. Until one day the translator was no longer needed and the sentences were mine.

First photo

The voice had a face

It is a strange thing to meet someone you already know. Then came Discord, then the calls, then the first photograph. I remember thinking the face did not match the voice, in the best way.

First call

Nowhere to hide an accent

On a call there is nowhere to hide your accent. I nodded too much and spoke too little. She talked; I pretended to follow. Then I went and learned the language properly, so I would not have to pretend.

Official

One night the call didn't end

She used to call me her 2D boyfriend. Then the evening calls turned into night calls, and the night calls turned into sleeping with the line open. Nobody decided it. It just stopped ending.

Every night

One word for a bad line

We sleep on a call. When the connection drops, we say one word — carrot — and it means: the line is bad, but I am still here.

Different countries, same heartbeat. The call only stops when she is at work. We eat on it. We shower on it. It is not really about talking.

Growing

Two people getting better

We are both working on how we think — the machinery under the thoughts. That is the part I am proudest of. Not the romance. The fact that we make each other better, and neither of us pretends the work is easy.

The plan

One piece of paper

Everything narrows to a single document now. We have named the cities, the months, the order in which things happen. The rest is waiting on a stamp.

Mexico

Not the same as a screen

Everything we had practised on a call — the rhythm, the pauses, the jokes — survived the landing. What did not survive was the distance. That part was over the moment she let go.

A letter

Carmen,

For years before I met you, my password was delacarmen. I had no idea whose name it was. It was yours: Carbajal and Mendoza make car-men. I have stopped calling that a coincidence.

I do not have to explain myself to you. You understand what I mean before I finish the sentence. You once asked if I had hacked you. I had not. You simply think the way I do.

You draw and you paint, and I watch. I am your most stubborn fan, and I will keep saying it until you believe me.

Five months. Long enough to know. Short enough that we are still at the beginning.

The pair

The dog and the rabbit

In the old Chinese reckoning I am the dog — loyal, stubborn, quick to guard the door. She is the rabbit — gentle, watchful, the one in the family who actually makes things. The texts call it one of the pairs that hold, because each brings what the other lacks.

I do not need a book to believe it. But it is nice that the book agrees.
