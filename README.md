# 💜 For Pocha — the archive

A private archive of one long-distance story between Mexico 🇲🇽 and Canada 🇨🇦:
two screens, a plane, and one door left — behind a password, with one page at the
end that is hers to write.

Published with GitHub Pages at `https://wildpig94.github.io/Pochy/`.
The page, the photographs and the two printable sheets are **encrypted**; the
URL alone shows a password box and nothing else. The password is four letters,
it is not written anywhere in this repository, and it is not reused for anything
else.

## 🔒 The lock

The site is published encrypted. What GitHub Pages serves is:

- `index.html` — the lock: a four-letter box and the decryption. **Generated, do
  not edit by hand.**
- `cifrado/` — the page, every photograph (originals and light versions) and the
  two PDFs, as AES-GCM ciphertext (PBKDF2-SHA256, 300 000 rounds).

Without the password, the repository shows file names and nothing else. The
password is normalised to lowercase and trimmed, so `toky`, `TOKY` and ` Toky `
all open the same thing.

The file you edit is **`fuente/index.html`**, in the clear. It is gitignored and
never published: only `cifrado/pagina.enc` goes out.

## 📴 Offline

The site installs as an app and opens without signal — on a plane, in a bad
signal, in the middle of the wait.

`sw.js` (generated by `tools/offline.mjs`) keeps a copy of the lock, the
encrypted page, the **light** photographs and the songs: about 10 MB. The
full-size originals, the two PDFs and the Google fonts are saved the first time
they are opened, and after that they are there too.

Consequences worth knowing:

- The **first** visit has to be online, and it has to be unlocked at least once
  so the encrypted page is stored.
- After a change, the cache is replaced on the next visit online (the version is
  a hash of the file list). There is no stale copy problem.
- On iPhone, *Share → Add to Home Screen*; on Android, the install prompt.

## ✨ What's inside

- 📖 **Seven chapters** — opened one at a time from the contents list.
- ⭐ **Under the same sky** — animated starfield with shooting stars.
- 💍 **The ring** — the T.O.K.Y. promise, one letter at a time.
- 🙈 **Hidden texts** — words in the story, a moon and two sparkles open a photo
  and a note.
- 📸 **The plates** — 45 photographs in their own appendix chapter, with no
  captions at all, grouped by the part of the wait they belong to; full size on
  tap, arrows and Escape on a keyboard.
- ♫ **Three songs** with lyrics timed to the audio.
- 🎟️ **Eleven coupons in their own chapter**, each with terms and conditions, a
  Redeem button that stamps it REDEEMED (kept in the browser), and a gold
  *Comodín del Comodín* sealed until the other ten are redeemed. Plus a **print
  room** and two ready-made PDFs she can print from any phone:
  `pdf/cupones.pdf` and `pdf/fotos.pdf`.
- 🦎 **The axolotl** — a small mascot in the corner. It talks when tapped, and it
  knows what has been read and what is still waiting.
- ✍️ **Her page** — the last chapter is empty; what she writes is kept in her
  phone and can be sent back with one tap.
- 👏 **The fan** — a block in chapter III that says why, with its own six
  photographs (the `FAN` list in `tools/fotos.py`).
- 🤫 **10-minute secret** — a toast that only shows up if she stays that long.

## ♫ Music

Three songs, two sources:

| # | Song | Local file | YouTube |
|---|------|-----------|---------|
| 01 | ADMV — Maluma | `audio/admv.m4a` | `wo7PBOCie9w` |
| 02 | Perdonar y Agradecer — Sharif | `audio/perdonar-y-agradecer.m4a` | `b31goMKtEXc` |
| 03 | Si Tú No Vuelves — Miguel Bosé | `audio/si-tu-no-vuelves.m4a` *(falta)* | `uWQI0o0Xv_U` |

1. If the file exists in `audio/`, it plays from there — no ads, no data, and the
   lyrics stay in sync because they are timed against the audio itself. Either
   extension works, the player tries `.m4a` first and then `.mp3`.
   What ships now is AAC 128 kbps with `faststart`.
2. If the file is missing, that song falls back to the YouTube player on its own.
   **Si Tú No Vuelves** is in that state: it plays from YouTube until the file is
   dropped in `audio/si-tu-no-vuelves.m4a` (and it is the one song without timed
   lyrics, because the lyrics are timed against the file).
3. `?source=yt` on the URL forces YouTube even when the files are there.

To add the missing file:

```
ffmpeg -i "Si tu no vuelves.mp3" -vn -c:a aac -b:a 128k -movflags +faststart \
  audio/si-tu-no-vuelves.m4a
```

Then, if you want the timed lyrics too, add the array next to `ADMV` and
`PERDONAR` inside `fuente/index.html` and list it in `LYRICS`. A song without an
array is fine: the lyrics panel simply stays closed.

The lyrics are timed against the studio cuts. If a version's intro runs longer,
correct it with `offset` (seconds, positive delays the lyrics). The YouTube
player ignores `offset`.

## 📸 Photographs

Originals live in **`fotos/`**; `memoria1` … `memoria26` are the timeline,
`nueva01` … `nueva14` the newer ones, `cap4-1` … `cap4-8` the last chapter, and
`nuestra-primera-foto.jpg` the one in the ring reveal.

`fotos/` is **not published in the clear**: it is gitignored, and what goes out
is `cifrado/fotos/…` only. The page never loads the originals while reading; it
loads the light versions in `fotos/web/` (480 px and 900 px WebP) and only
reaches for the original when a photo is opened full size or printed.

### The photographs live in their own chapter

No photograph carries a caption. The story is text; the photographs are in
**chapter VII — The plates**, grouped by the part of the wait they belong to and
in the order they arrived. A group label can be wrong, but a group label does not
sit on top of one photograph pretending to describe it.

Which group each photograph is in lives in **one place**: `GRUPOS` in
`tools/fotos.py`. From that table one command writes the gallery:

```
python3 tools/fotos.py
```

- rewrites the gallery inside `fuente/index.html`, between the `FOTOS:inicio` and
  `FOTOS:fin` markers, with srcset and `data-full` already built — and if nothing
  changed in the table the file comes out byte for byte identical;
- builds **`revisar.html`**, a local page with every photograph, its file name
  and a dropdown to move it to another group. It is gitignored and never
  published.

When you add a photograph: run `python3 tools/webp.py`, add its line to a group
in the table, and run `python3 tools/fotos.py`.

## 🖨️ Printing

Printing from a phone through the browser's print dialog is where it broke, so
the two fixed sheets are real PDFs:

- `pdf/cupones.pdf` — the eleven coupons with the terms opened, one per row,
  Letter size, four pages.
- `pdf/fotos.pdf` — all 45 photographs, two per row, 12 pages.

They are encrypted like everything else: the two links in the page go through the
lock and hand back the decrypted file. Rebuild them after changing the coupons or
adding photographs:

```
node tools/imprimir.mjs
```

It drives its own headless Chrome over **`fuente/index.html`** (so the sheet
matches the site's styles) and writes both files. The print room's "Print
selected" still uses `window.print()` for a custom pick.

## 🦎 The axolotl

Lives at the bottom left, fixed, so it never covers text. It blinks, breathes,
and when tapped says something about what is still unread. It lifts a small heart
when a hidden text is found, and it hides when printing.

## ✍️ Her page

Chapter VI. It autosaves to `localStorage` as she types (a small "kept" flashes
for two seconds and then goes), and **Send it to me** opens the share sheet (or
WhatsApp) with the text already written. Nothing leaves her phone until she
presses send.

## 🎟️ The coupons

Each card opens its own **Terms** (a `<details>`, so the fine print is one tap
away, like a real contract). **Redeem** asks for confirmation, slams the stamp on
the card and keeps it in `localStorage`. There is no WhatsApp and no network: the
coupon is redeemed in her browser and nowhere else.

No. 011 is sealed until Nos. 001–010 have been redeemed. Nothing is checked on a
server: the count lives in her browser.

## 🧠 What the page remembers

`localStorage`, key `pochy-archive-v1`: chapters opened, plates opened, hidden
texts found, her page, first and last visit. None of it is shown as a number —
there is no progress bar and no visit counter on purpose. What it feeds is the
returning-visitor line in the opening screen and the axolotl. Clearing browser
data resets all of it.

## 🚀 Edit and publish

Edit `fuente/index.html`. Then, always in this order:

```
python3 tools/fotos.py                 # si cambiaste fotos o grupos
node tools/imprimir.mjs                # si cambiaste cupones o fotos
node tools/candado.mjs 'la-contrasena' # fuente/ + fotos/ + pdf/ -> index.html + cifrado/
node tools/offline.mjs                 # cifrado/ + audio/ -> sw.js
git add -A && git commit -m "..." && git push
```

`tools/candado.mjs` keeps a manifest of what it already encrypted, so it only
re-encrypts what changed; the salt also stays the same, so the password does not
have to be re-keyed after every edit. GitHub Pages rebuilds in a minute or two.

To read the page without the lock while working (no encryption, plain photos),
open `fuente/index.html` directly: when `window.__abrir` does not exist, the page
falls back to the plain URLs — including the PDF links.

## 🗂️ Files

- `index.html` — **the lock**. Generated; do not edit by hand.
- `fuente/index.html` — the page itself, in the clear. **This is the file to
  edit.** Gitignored, never published.
- `cifrado/` — the encrypted page, the encrypted photographs and the two
  encrypted PDFs.
- `sw.js` — the offline cache list. Generated by `tools/offline.mjs`.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/` — so it installs as an app.
- `audio/` — the songs.
- `fotos/` — originals and light versions, in the clear. Gitignored, never
  published.
- `pdf/` — the two printable sheets, in the clear. Gitignored, never published;
  what goes out is `cifrado/pdf/`.
- `robots.txt` — keeps search engines out.
- `nueva.html`, `original.html` — old copies. Gitignored; safe to delete.

### Tools

- `tools/webp.py` — rebuilds `fotos/web/`.
- `tools/fotos.py` — the photograph/group table, and the script that writes the
  gallery into `fuente/index.html` and builds `revisar.html`.
- `tools/revisar.plantilla.html` — the shell of the review page.
- `tools/candado.mjs` — encrypts the page, the photographs and the PDFs, and
  writes the lock.
- `tools/candado.plantilla.html` — the lock's own HTML.
- `tools/offline.mjs` + `tools/sw.plantilla.js` — build `sw.js`.
- `tools/imprimir.mjs` — builds the two PDFs from `fuente/index.html`.

## 🔐 A note on the history

Until the lock came back, the page, the photographs and the PDFs were public and
unencrypted, and `fotos/` and `pdf/` were committed in the clear. They are no
longer in the current tree — the working copies stay on this machine and are
gitignored — but **they are still in the older commits**. If that matters, the
history has to be rewritten (`git filter-repo`) and force-pushed; that breaks any
existing clone, so it is a decision to take on purpose, not a side effect.
