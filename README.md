# 💜 For Pocha — Our First 3 Months

A romantic, interactive webpage for Carmen ("Pocha") from Pocho (Alexis), celebrating the first 3 months of a long-distance relationship between Mexico 🇲🇽 and Canada 🇨🇦 — and counting down to the day they finally meet in person.

## ✨ Features
- 💜 **Purple theme** — her favorite color.
- 🔒 **Password gate** — only she can enter (the answer is what the ring says).
- ✦ **Start button** — an intro overlay to open the experience.
- ⏱️ **Countdown** — live countdown to the moment they meet at the airport.
- ⭐ **"Under the same sky"** — animated starry night sky with shooting stars.
- 💥 **Heart burst** on entry + hearts that follow the cursor.
- 📖 **Chapters** — the story opens one chapter at a time.
- 💍 **Ring reveal** — the T.O.K.Y. promise, one letter at a time.
- 📸 **Polaroid photo gallery** — photos interleaved through the timeline (tap to enlarge).
- 🙈 **Six hidden texts** — the highlighted words in the story open a photo and a note.
- ✉️ **Envelope letter** — the final love letter opens like a real envelope.
- ⌨️ **Typewriter** hero text + a surprise button at the end.
- 🤫 **10-minute secret** — a toast that only shows up if she stays that long.
- ♫ **Music** — plays 3 songs in order, then loops.

## 📸 Photos
Photos live in the **`fotos/`** folder. `memoria1` … `memoria26` are the timeline,
`nueva01` … `nueva14` are the newer ones used by the hidden texts and the moments
gallery, and `nuestra-primera-foto.jpg` is the one in the ring reveal. To replace
one, overwrite the file keeping the same name. The gallery grouping is defined in
`IMG_GROUPS` in `index.html`.
